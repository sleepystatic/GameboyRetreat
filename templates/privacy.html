Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Gameboy Retreat is a one-person shop in Stockton, California. This page says plainly what the site collects, why, and who else handles it. Your personal information is never sold.

What is collected

When you buy something

Your name, email address and shipping address, plus what you bought and what you paid. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout and never reach this site's servers.

When you offer a console to sell

Your email address and what you tell us about the item: the console, its condition, your asking price and whether you prefer pickup or shipping.

When you join the email list

Your email address. It is used only to send new restorations and shop news, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

When you browse

Which pages you view, which buttons you click, and which site sent you here (for example a search engine or a link posted on social media). This is tied to a random identifier kept in your browser for that visit only. It is not linked to your name or email, no cookie is set for it, and it is deleted after 180 days.

The site also uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and collects information such as your device type and approximate location. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's privacy policy, and you can opt out with Google's browser add-on.

How it is used

  • To ship your order and handle warranty claims and returns
  • To reply when you contact us or offer an item for sale
  • To send the email updates you signed up for
  • To understand which pages and products are useful, so the shop improves

Who else processes it

Only services the shop needs to run:

  • Stripe — payments
  • Supabase — the database and product photo storage
  • Resend — sending email
  • Render — hosting the website
  • Google — Google Analytics

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from email at any time from the link in any message. To see what is held about you, or to have it deleted, email {{ support_email }}. Order records may be kept where they are needed for tax or warranty purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: {{ support_email }}
