What "refurbished" means here

Every console is sourced as a non-working or worn unit and brought back by hand: thoroughly cleaned inside and out, rehoused in a new shell, and any faults repaired so it works like new. Nothing ships until it has been tested and plays properly.

Returns

If a console arrives faulty or is not as described, contact us within 30 days for a replacement or full refund. Return shipping is on us when the fault is ours.
