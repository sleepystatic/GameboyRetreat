The restoration process

Most listings that say refurbished mean wiped with a cloth. Here is what actually happens, on every single unit.

  1. 01

    Fully stripped

    Board out of the shell. Nothing gets cleaned around.

  2. 02

    Deep cleaned

    Board cleaned properly and corrosion treated at the battery terminals before it eats the traces underneath.

  3. 03

    Rehoused

    New shell, new buttons, new membranes. It should feel new, not just look it.

  4. 04

    Faults repaired

    Screens, audio, charge ports — actually fixed, not worked around or disclosed away in the listing.

  5. 05

    Played, then shipped

    A real session on real hardware. If it would annoy me, it does not go out.
